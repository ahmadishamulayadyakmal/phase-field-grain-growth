# Grain morphology and dendrite control (C08)

## Industrial problem

As-cast grain size and dendrite arm spacing set downstream strength and toughness; weld and additive-manufacturing thermal histories produce microstructures a plant cannot afford to trial-and-error.

**Why it happens.** Grain growth and dendrite selection depend nonlinearly on the thermal gradient and cooling rate supplied by the process-scale model.

## Data (no plant data)

Open data only: PRISMS-PF benchmark cases (R22, open source) and the analytical grain-growth law `d^n - d0^n = Kt` (R24). Thermal fields come from C07, not from a plant.

> Only open datasets and published literature are used. No plant or proprietary data are held here; plant numbers quoted from papers are external literature, not independently reproduced results.

## Tool chain

Phase-field (in-house + PRISMS-PF comparison), coupling from C07 thermal history, JMAK kinetics, mesh convergence, V&V note, script-generated figures.

## Journal references

- **R21** — solidification microstructure bridge.
- **R22** ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-020-0298-5)) — PRISMS-PF framework and cross-code comparator.
- **R23** — phase-field model review.
- **R24** — analytical grain-growth law for verification (compound: 2 papers).
- **R26** — JMAK kinetics (compound: 2 papers).

## Deliverables

Grain-growth and dendrite cases; PRISMS-PF comparison; coupling report; V&V note; figure set generated by script.

## Validation & claims

Recovered growth exponent matches the analytical law within tolerance; mesh convergence shown; coupling from a C07 field runs end-to-end from one command.

## Limitations

2D, idealised binary alloy. No plant microstructure images are used.
