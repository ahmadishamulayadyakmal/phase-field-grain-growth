**Repository:** `e08-phase-field-microstructure` · **Evidence: A** ·
**References:** R21, R22, R23, R24.

**Industrial problem.** As-cast grain size and dendrite arm spacing set downstream
strength and toughness; weld and AM thermal histories produce microstructures
that a plant cannot afford to trial-and-error.

**Why it happens.** Grain growth and dendrite selection depend nonlinearly on the
thermal gradient and cooling rate supplied by the process-scale model.

**Data (open, no plant data).** PRISMS-PF benchmark cases (R22, open source) and
the analytical grain-growth law `dⁿ − d₀ⁿ = Kt` (R24). Thermal fields are taken
from C07, not from a plant.

**Tool chain.** phase-field (in-house + PRISMS-PF comparison) → coupling from C07
thermal history → grain-growth-law verification → mesh convergence → V&V note.

**What each reference validates.**
- R22 — PRISMS-PF framework and cross-code comparator.
- R23 — phase-field model review.
- R24 — analytical grain-growth law for verification.
- R21 — solidification microstructure bridge.

**Deliverables.** grain-growth and dendrite cases; PRISMS-PF comparison;
coupling report; V&V note; figure set generated by script.

**Validation.** Recovered growth exponent matches the analytical law within
tolerance; mesh convergence shown; 2D/idealised-binary limitation stated.

**Acceptance.** Coupling from a C07 field runs end-to-end from one command.
